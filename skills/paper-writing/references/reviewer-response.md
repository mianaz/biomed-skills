# Response to reviewers

Read the decision letter, complete reviewer comments, designated manuscript
version and records of revisions actually completed. Follow supplied response
formatting and numbering. Open with a concise account of the major changes and
their scientific value.

Keep each reviewer comment identifiable and answer its scientific concern directly.
For each point, connect:

- The question or concern raised.
- The answer supported by the available evidence and work completed.
- The resulting manuscript change and its verified section, figure or line location.

For a compound comment, cover each consequential part. Include enough of the new
result or explanation to make the answer understandable. Put important new results,
methods or clarifications into the relevant manuscript section when revision is
in scope. If the manuscript is unavailable, supply the proposed insertion and mark
its location for confirmation. Describe proposed work with its actual status.

When a request is addressed by explanation or a change in interpretation, state
the scientific rationale and the revision clearly. Preserve the contribution while
aligning the specific claim with the evidence. Use a separate editor note for
confidential or submission-scope issues when that communication is requested.

Before delivery, match responses against the full comment list, completed work
and revised manuscript. Verify every claimed modification and locator. Deliver
the response letter and any requested manuscript changes. `paper-polishing` edits
an existing response within its agreed scope; `submission-check` checks the
revision package and current journal requirements.
