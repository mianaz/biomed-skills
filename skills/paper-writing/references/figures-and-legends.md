# Figure legends

Reuse the established figure sequence and scientific role of each figure. Read
the actual panels, source results and group definitions before writing captions.
Identify the finding the legend should help the reader understand.

Orient the reader to the scientific comparison and explain panels in their
existing order. Include group definitions, sample units, n, summaries, error bars,
tests, multiplicity handling and exclusions when relevant and supported. Match
each statement to the displayed evidence. Keep terminology and figure references
consistent with the manuscript. State essential quantitative information exactly.

For a conceptual model, distinguish established observations from proposed links
in the wording and key. Explain the model's scientific meaning at its supported
scope. Check the legend against the finished figure and the relevant Methods.

An overall story or figure-order decision uses paper-framing. Rendering, panel
layout, color, fonts and statistical display use scientific-plotting. Within the
writing task, deliver the legend and identify any consequential figure mismatch.
