# Paper Writing

Read [shared principles](../biomed-shared/core-principles.md).

## Establish the purpose

Read [writing principles](../biomed-shared/writing-principles.md). Use the supplied
evidence, designated manuscript version and established decisions. Reuse framing,
claim IDs and terminology when present; develop the argument directly when a
brief is absent. Identify the question, finding and interpretation the reader
should understand, with source support for consequential statements.

## Draft and deliver

Build the requested prose around discoveries: local interpretation in Results,
integration across findings in Discussion. Attach numerical evidence, citations
and figures to the claims they support. Convert fragmentary Chinese notes into
natural biomedical English when requested. Use placeholders for missing facts.

Check scientific interest, factual meaning, numbers and source correspondence.
For a full manuscript or drafting/rebuilding an argument across paragraphs, use
[logic review](../biomed-shared/logic-review.md) and resolve material findings.
Short titles, abstracts, legends and faithful Methods transcription use direct
checks unless independent review is requested. At manuscript scope, use [reader read-back](../biomed-shared/reader-feedback.md) to check what the
title, abstract, headings and figures communicate. Finish with usable prose and
consequential notes; provide an outline when that is the requested product.

For an explicit choice of scientific story, use `paper-framing`; for independent
editing that preserves the claims and reasoning chain, including paragraph
reordering or faithful translation, use `paper-polishing`.
`protocol-to-methods` establishes Methods from execution records. `paper-review`,
`citation-audit` and `submission-check` own their expressly requested assessments
or packages. Complete each requested product and reuse its source records.
