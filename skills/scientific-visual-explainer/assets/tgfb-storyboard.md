# TGFβ1–SMAD3/SMAD4 · storyboard

Audience: biomedical researchers. Goal: follow a membrane receptor signal through local phosphorylation, stoichiometric assembly and nuclear transcription. Use the same editable SVGs in the complete figure and the animation.

| Time (s) | Question | Visible change | Takeaway | Source key |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0–4 | What recognizes mature ligand? | TGFβ1 dimer docks to receptor II | Ligand recognition precedes receptor I activation | receptor |
| 4–8 | How is a receptor pair organized? | Second receptor II arrives laterally | A dimer supplies two receptor-II contacts | assembly |
| 8–12 | Where does receptor I enter? | Two ALK5 molecules join the same complex | Two type-I and two type-II receptors | assembly |
| 12–14 | What is modified first? | GS P circles appear on ALK5 | Receptor II phosphorylates receptor I | gs |
| 14–26 | How is the signal passed on? | Two SMAD3 molecules contact ALK5, gain two P circles and release | C-terminal S423/S425 are substrate states | smad-p |
| 26–34 | What assembles? | Two pSMAD3 molecules dock on opposite SMAD4 MH2 surfaces | A 2:1 heterotrimer | trimer |
| 34–44 | What crosses the nuclear boundary? | The linked three-component cargo and four P circles traverse the open pore | Assembly and modification persist through import | import |
| 44–50 | Where does the cargo act? | The trimer clears the pore, then approaches DNA from below | Generic response-element contact, not sequence-specific artwork | dna |
| 50–58 | What is the output? | RNA appears beside the bound complex; complete figure holds | Transcriptional response requires co-regulators combined here | transcription |

Controls: ALK5 catalytic activity blocked (16 s) retains GS-P but no downstream SMAD3 P/RNA; SMAD3 S423A/S425A (28 s) retains receptor GS-P and substrate contact, with no SMAD3 P, trimer, import or RNA in this teaching branch. These are qualitative mechanism comparisons, not quantitative inhibitor or mutant experiments.

SMAD2 is a library-only extension. It is not used as the direct DNA-binding SMAD3 actor. Geometry, relative sizes, assembly order and timing are schematic. Latent-ligand activation, SARA, feedback, detailed transport factors, co-regulators and RNA polymerase are combined or omitted. See references.json for exact source links and reading scope.
