# Wnt pathway icon QC

These 10 editable teaching vectors use the shared 160 × 160 canvas and v0.3 style. Their paths are original and covered by the package MIT license. No BioRender artwork or vectors were copied.

## Visual design reference

[BioRender Wnt/β-catenin pathway template](https://www.biorender.com/template/wnt-beta-catenin-signaling-pathway) is the sole visual-design reference for this set. Its public rendered preview was inspected for silhouette and pathway-layout conventions. It is proprietary reference material; the delivered SVG geometry is original.

## Scientific QC references

These papers support pathway and domain content. They are kept separate from the visual-design reference.

| Paper | Inspection and scientific scope |
|---|---|
| [Colozza & Koo, Wnt signaling review, Fig. 1](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8251975/) | Figure visually inspected for ligand–receptor and destruction-complex relationships. |
| [Wnt–Frizzled structural paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5727331/) | Domain and topology descriptions consulted for Wnt–FZD interface context; source coordinates were not traced. |

## Feature and render checks

| Icon | Scientific feature checked |
|---|---|
| Wnt ligand | Cystine-rich domains, palmitoleate-bearing thumb, FZD and LRP6 contact sides |
| Frizzled | Extracellular CRD, seven membrane passes, cytoplasmic DVL tail |
| LRP6 | Four YWTD propeller–EGF units, three LDLR-A modules, membrane helix, PPPSP tail |
| Dishevelled | Continuous DIX–PDZ–DEP chain and interaction surfaces |
| Axin | RGS and DIX ends joined by a continuous scaffold |
| APC | Oligomerization region, armadillo block, β-catenin repeats, SAMP repeats and connected C-terminal arm |
| GSK3β / CK1α | Bilobal kinase silhouettes and cleft-facing surfaces |
| β-catenin | Armadillo-repeat body, phosphodegron, TCF groove and activation tail |
| TCF/LEF | HMG-box contour, β-catenin interaction tail and removable DNA context |

Prototype CairoSVG renders at 64, 96 and 160 px were visually checked. Validation confirms 10 manifest entries; matching named SVG parts; allowed palette; 160 × 160 viewBoxes; unit anchor normals; anchors on rendered artwork; no embedded text, images or filters; and alpha bounds inside the canvas with margin.
