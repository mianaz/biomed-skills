# Generated TGFβ–SMAD family · v0.5

Six accepted built-in imagegen PNGs were traced into editable SVG paths: mature TGFβ1 dimer, TGFβRII, TGFβRI/ALK5, SMAD3, SMAD4 and SMAD2. Exact initial and correction prompts are in prompts.json; accepted source PNGs are in sources/. SMAD3/SMAD2 were simplified through the built-in editor to remove excess ribbon detail. Colors, domain groups and anchors are editable; no PNG is embedded in any icon SVG.

## References inspected

Visual: the full public BioRender TGF-beta signaling template preview was inspected in the browser for flat composition and detail level. No BioRender artwork was copied or traced. Its URL is retained in each manifest entry.

Scientific: references.json records primary abstracts, available indexed captions and the exact access limits. Receptor assembly follows Radaev 2010 (PMID 20207738) and Zúñiga 2005 (16289576); GS-region activation follows Wieser 1995 (7774578); SMAD phosphorylation follows Liu 1997 (9380693). Two phosphorylated R-SMAD subunits plus one SMAD4 follow Chacko 2004 (15350224). Transport, DNA and transcription references are linked separately. Direct access to the RBPMS full text returned a CAPTCHA; its S423/S425 mutant description was read from a search-indexed primary excerpt, not the full paper. This is not a full structure/figure audit.

## Contact and motion inspection

Three shared schematic contour profiles support ligand–RII, ligand–RI and MH2 association at the declared assembly scales. Symmetric duplicate ports share one profile, and the complementary edge is fitted into the partner SVG. Original traced bodies and local vector clips remain editable. Profile samples match at bound/reaction-contact states, including both SMAD4 sides and ALK5 substrate contact. This does not reconstruct an atomic interface.

The 58-second lesson uses four membrane-anchored receptors, two pSMAD3 and one SMAD4. The whole linked cargo, including P circles, passes through the reused nuclear-pore icon. DNA and RNA were reused in v0.5; v0.6 replaces them with generated nucleic-imagegen assets. The DNA surface-contact anchor leaves the generic double helix intact.

At 0.1-second samples across three conditions (1,023 states), actual SVG silhouette checks found no text/text, text/body, arrow/body, arrow/text or badge collisions. There were no filled-body overlaps above 24 square drawing units between unrelated components, and no such overlap in completed contacts. During lateral second-RII recruitment only (5.7–6.4 s), the intended ligand–receptor pair has a brief contact-area overlap, maximum 128 square drawing units; it resolves before docking completes. All raw detections remain in the development layout report. This is a schematic assembly cue, not a rigid-body molecular simulation.

All 132 library SVGs passed palette, canvas, local clips, named parts, anchor-normal and alpha-bound checks. The 190 mm wide static SVG/PDF uses Arial, minimum 10.95 pt. Twenty-seven full states were rendered for review; beginning, receptor reaction, assembled cargo, nuclear exit, DNA-bound final and control frames were visually inspected. Live browser playback/control scope is recorded in validation.md.
