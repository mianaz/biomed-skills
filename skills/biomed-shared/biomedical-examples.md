# Biomedical framing examples

All scenarios below are fictional teaching examples. They are not evidence for
any real molecule, treatment, disease mechanism or manuscript. Use the supplied
facts of the actual study when drafting.

## A discovery whose value is understated

**Hypothetical evidence:** A treatment induces a cell state that persists after
washout. Functional assays show that cells retaining this state respond
differently to a subsequent challenge. The molecular basis of persistence has
not been determined.

**Timid draft:** “Our study only describes changes in cell state and does not
fully establish the underlying mechanism.”

**Developed framing:** “Treatment establishes a persistent cell state that
alters the response to a subsequent challenge. This persistence suggests that
prior exposure changes how cells interpret later signals. We propose that the
state carries a form of cellular memory, raising the question of which regulatory
features retain and transmit that memory.”

The observed persistence and altered response are stated directly. The broader
model is proposed explicitly and developed into a biological question.

## An incomplete mechanism with a coherent interpretation

**Hypothetical evidence:** Disrupting cofactor Q reduces transcription factor R
occupancy and its target transcription in the tested cells. Direct physical
interaction and the order of recruitment have not been tested.

**Developed framing:** “Cofactor Q supports transcription factor R occupancy
and target transcription in these cells. Together, these findings support a
model in which Q helps establish a chromatin environment permissive for R
activity. This model predicts that restoring chromatin accessibility would
partially recover R occupancy after Q disruption.”

The final sentence is a model prediction. Include it only as a proposal, with
reasoning appropriate to the actual measurements. Direct recruitment would
require a different statement and supporting evidence.

## Heterogeneity that reveals a useful biological distinction

**Hypothetical evidence:** A perturbation changes an outcome in cell state A,
with little change in state B. The comparison was measured with suitable
replication in both states.

**Timid draft:** “Unfortunately, the effect was not consistent in all cells.”

**Developed framing:** “The response depends on cell state, identifying state A
as selectively sensitive to the perturbation. This selectivity suggests that
the underlying dependency is acquired during the transition into state A.”

The observed selectivity is the result. Acquisition during a transition is an
interpretation to examine against temporal or mechanistic evidence. Develop that
specific question rather than treating every heterogeneous effect as a failure.

## A valuable method assembled from established components

**Hypothetical evidence:** A combined assay distinguishes two measured responses
that a conventional single readout conflates. This changes the interpretation of
several treatments in the tested system.

**Developed framing:** “Joint measurement resolves treatment responses that
share a conventional readout but arise from distinct cellular changes. The assay
therefore enables a more informative interpretation of perturbation responses
and reveals why similar aggregate signals can conceal different biological
behaviors.”

Explain what the combined measurement enables and what was learned. Attribute
established components accurately. Keep claims about other systems tied to
actual testing or express them as proposed uses.

## A null result whose meaning depends on the design

**Hypothetical evidence:** A perturbation changes one measured process. A second
outcome has an uncertain estimate that includes biologically relevant effects.

**Appropriate framing:** State the positive finding directly. Report the estimate
and uncertainty for the second outcome where it affects the interpretation.
The second result does not establish equivalence or the absence of an effect.
A model of selective regulation can be discussed as a hypothesis only when its
reasoning and unresolved distinction are clear.

Scientific ambition comes from a useful interpretation of the available
observations. Numerical uncertainty must retain its actual meaning.

## Revision when the user cannot add experiments

Work from the existing manuscript, figures and analyses. Find the most important
result, connect complementary observations and explain the emerging biological
model. Improve figure order, titles, local interpretation and Discussion. Use
existing data for additional analysis when the task permits it.

Complete the requested manuscript revision. When future work is requested,
identify the experiment that would most clearly distinguish the competing
biological explanations and explain that prediction. Preserve the status of
that experiment as proposed.
