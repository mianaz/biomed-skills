# Biomedical writing principles

Use for biomedical prose, with the shared principles and the requested edit scope.

1. **Lead with the scientific contribution.** Develop the strongest biological
   story supported by the findings: what is surprising, what understanding changes
   and why it matters. Check for underselling as deliberately as for overstatement.
2. **Combine precise facts with ambitious interpretation.** Use biological reasoning
   to develop mechanisms, models, implications and productive questions when the
   task calls for them. State proposals clearly and qualify the specific uncertainty
   that affects the claim. An open mechanism can sustain an important contribution.
3. **Write for understanding.** Organize the text around discoveries and their
   meaning. Turn working records into a reader-facing argument; keep operational
   history in linked records. Use concrete subjects, clear relationships and established terms.
   Preserve effective author voice; remove repetition, forced contrasts and
   reflexive defensive wording.
4. **Deliver useful prose.** Return the requested text first. Add concise author
   notes only for consequential changes, unresolved facts or decisions.
