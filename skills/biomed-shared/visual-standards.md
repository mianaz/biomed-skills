# Biomedical visual standards

Use for figure artwork and assembly. These are shared visual defaults; the active
skill routes to its layout, software, export and verification instructions.
Manuscript body text follows its document format.

## 1. Scientific typography and effective size

Use Arial and correct entity/species nomenclature: complete gene symbols in
italics, proteins and units upright, with proper mathematical notation. Target
text above 10 pt at final placement; dense plot labels have an 8 pt floor.
Measure effective size after all placement scales and inspect the saved view.

Name biological quantities clearly and preserve their units and scientific meaning.
Keep exact computation details in the caption or linked source record.

## 2. Physical space and panel dimensions

Start ordinary single data panels within a 50 × 50 mm complete canvas, including
labels and legends. Remove unused space and resolve crowding before enlarging the
necessary dimension. Preserve useful data-panel height, scientific grouping and
the aspect ratio of imported panels. Whole figures, schematics and lessons use
dimensions appropriate to their requested format and content. Apply task-specific
content and legibility requirements before the compact-canvas starting preference.

## 3. Semantic color and contrast

Honor explicit user palettes (package, named palette or hex values), then existing
project mappings. Otherwise select colors for the variable and reading task.
Use a sequential scale for ordered magnitude and a diverging scale for
a meaningful center. Keep category identity and comparable scales stable; state
limits and reference points. Schematic symbols follow the selected schematic style; embedded data plots
retain their quantitative color meaning. Check legibility in the final view.

## 4. Independent panel and letter contract

Export individual panels for manuscript assembly without publication letters.
Keep scientific labels inside each panel. In an editable manuscript assembly,
retain independent panel assets and separate editable panel-letter text boxes.
