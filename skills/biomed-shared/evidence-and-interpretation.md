# Evidence and interpretation

Read the supplied text, actual figure panels, legends and relevant Methods before
assessing a scientific claim. Record the accessible material once. Give each
consequential claim a source locator and preserve the comparison, independent
unit, effect direction and uncertainty needed to interpret it. An abstract
supports an assessment at that scope. Check the closest relevant primary papers
before a firm novelty comparison; mark unverified comparisons as provisional.

Separate the measured observation, the relationship established by the design,
and the proposed explanation. Association, intervention, necessity, sufficiency,
mediation and direct interaction answer different questions. A perturbation or
rescue supports the inference identified by its controls and context; direct
binding and downstream mediation require evidence specific to those relationships.
A nonsignificant estimate with a wide interval does not establish equivalence.

Develop a proposed mechanism through explicit biological reasoning. State its
informative prediction and distinguish the proposal from a reported result.
Keep contradictory findings where they affect interpretation. Preserve the status
of exploratory analyses, planned work and completed experiments through revisions.
