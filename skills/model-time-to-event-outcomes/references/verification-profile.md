# Time-to-event verification profile

## Cohort construction

- Eligibility, assignment/exposure, time zero, delayed entry, and follow-up are ordered
  coherently for every analyzed unit.
- Eligible, excluded, missing, entered, event, competing-event, censored, and analyzed
  counts reconcile by group and important site/period.
- Covariates are available at their declared time and no future information or immortal
  time defines a baseline group.

## Event process

- Event, competing event, censoring, recurrence, and terminal-event rules are explicit
  and mutually coherent.
- Reported horizons have adequate risk-set support; median survival is reported only
  when estimable.
- Kaplan-Meier, cumulative incidence, and censoring treatment match the stated quantity.

## Model and statistics

- Estimand, time scale, formula, contrasts, clustering, adjustment set, missingness,
  and multiplicity family are recoverable.
- Model assumptions, functional form, influence, sparse risk sets, and absolute-risk
  calibration are checked as applicable.
- Effects, intervals, p-values, event counts, denominators, and direction agree across
  tables, figures, and claims.

## Sensitivity and claim

- Material time-origin, exposure, competing-risk, censoring, missingness, and influence
  sensitivities are reported or justified as not applicable.
- Hazard, risk, restricted-time, predictive, and causal claims are not interchanged.
